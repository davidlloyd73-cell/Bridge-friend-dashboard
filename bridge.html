    Efficiency

    All-time
    # Player Total HCP HCP / hand Efficiency

    Efficiency = total score ÷ total HCP dealt: points won per high-card point held. Higher means you squeezed more out of the cards you were given. HCP / hand is the average strength of your hands — if two players differ there, the deal has been kinder to one of them. Ranked by efficiency.

    Partnerships

    Pair Hands Won Avg points

    Every pairing, all-time, counting each hand once for each pair at the table. A pair wins a hand when its side scored more than the other side; passed-out hands are left out. Avg points is what the pair’s side scored per hand. Best win rate in green, worst in red.

    Contracts

    All-time

    Contracts made out of contracts declared, by kind. Game is 3NT, 4 of a major or 5 of a minor; below that is a part-score. Doubled counts doubled and redoubled contracts of any kind. Best rate green and worst red, only where at least three players have declared five or more of that kind. Favourite is the denomination each player declares most often.

    Quirks

    All-time

    Bidding: average tricks over or under the contract as declarer. Whoever takes the most spare tricks plays it safe; whoever falls shortest bids the moon. Defence: how often the opponents’ contract went down with this player defending; the best rate is the Wall. Late night: points per hand in the first half of each finished night against the second half; a gap of 15 or more earns a label. Rivals: your side’s points minus theirs, per hand, with that player sitting against you. The nemesis is the one you do worst against, even if you’re still ahead.

    Sheet check

    Every hand on record whose high card points don't add up. A deck holds 40, so four logged seats must total 40 — where they don't, one of the four is mistyped and only you can say which. Where exactly one seat is blank the deck settles it, and the value it must have held is shown as n? — a suggestion for the sheet, never counted in any total or average on this page.
